# Code of Conduct

Open Model Fabric adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, as its code of
conduct. The full text is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

## In short

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

Expected behaviour includes empathy and kindness, respect for differing opinions,
giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for
mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment,
publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct that could
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces and when someone officially represents the project
in public spaces. Report incidents privately to the maintainers, using the repository's
private vulnerability-reporting channel with a "Code of Conduct" title until a dedicated
address is published. All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's
privacy is respected. Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement
guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, and permanent ban.
